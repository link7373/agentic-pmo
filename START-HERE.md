> **Created by Colin Beck**<br>
> LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/beckcolin/<br>
> GitHub: https://github.com/link7373

Welcome. This file is the **one thing you fill in** to stand up your virtual Product & Project Management
Office. Answer in plain English — bullet points, brain dumps, half-formed thoughts are all fine. You don't
need to know any frameworks or jargon. If you don't know an answer, write "no idea" or leave it blank and
your PMO team will ask or use sensible defaults.

When you're done, run:

```
/setup-pmo
```

That reads your answers, sets up your team's memory, and gets everyone ready to work. You can always come
back and update this file later, then re-run `/setup-pmo`.

---

## 1. The Product / Portfolio
*What are you building, and where is it in its life?*

- What is the product (or products/projects) this PMO will manage?
- What stage is it at? (just an idea / building an MVP / launched / scaling)
- What's the big-picture vision — the change you want to create in the world?
- Are we managing one product, or a portfolio of several efforts?

_Your answer here..._

## 2. Users & Market
*Who is this for, and who else is out there?*

- Who are your target customers / users? Be as specific as you can.
- What problem are you solving for them? Why does it matter to them?
- Who are the main alternatives or competitors today (including "they do nothing")?
- Anything notable about your market (size, trends, regulation)?

_Your answer here..._

## 3. Goals & Metrics
*What does success look like this quarter / this year?*

- What are your top goals right now? (objectives or OKRs, if you have them)
- Is there a single "north star" number you care most about?
- How do you measure success today? What numbers do you watch?
- What's the most important question about the product you can't currently answer?

_Your answer here..._

## 4. Teams & Stakeholders
*Who's involved, and who decides?*

- Who's on the team(s)? Roles, rough headcount, how many delivery teams.
- Who are the key stakeholders / decision-makers / sponsors?
- Who has final say on scope and priorities?
- Any tricky stakeholder dynamics we should know about?

_Your answer here..._

## 5. Process & Cadence
*How does the work actually run?*

- How do you work today? (Scrum / Kanban / hybrid / ad hoc / not sure)
- If you do sprints — how long are they? Which ceremonies do you run?
- What's your release cadence? (continuous / weekly / monthly / big releases)
- Any deadlines, milestones, or fixed dates coming up?

_Your answer here..._

## 6. Tools & Integrations
*Where does the work live, and how should we output things?*

- What tools do you use for backlog/issues? (Jira / Linear / Notion / GitHub / spreadsheet / none)
- Where do you communicate? (Slack / Teams / email)
- Should the PMO **sync** with any of those tools, or just keep everything in files here?
  (Files are the default source of truth; syncing is optional and set up later.)
- Any formatting/branding needs for documents and reports?
- **Reporting platform for dashboards?** (Power BI is the default and the PMO can build those itself as
  projects; Tableau or anything else means it specs the dashboard and someone builds it.)
- **Brand colours** for dashboards, if you have them? (Otherwise sensible accessible defaults are used.)

_Your answer here..._

## 7. Rules & Boundaries
*What must we respect or avoid?*

- Any compliance, legal, security, or privacy requirements?
- Anything the PMO should **not** do without explicit approval?
- Budget, headcount, or timeline constraints we must work within?

_Your answer here..._

## 8. Context & Quirks
*The stuff that's obvious to you but not to a newcomer.*

- Internal terminology, acronyms, or product names we should learn.
- Timezone, working calendar, fiscal calendar, currency.
- History worth knowing — past pivots, sacred cows, lessons learned, "we tried that."

_Your answer here..._

## 9. Money, People & Governance
*Who approves what, and what are we working with?* "No idea" is a fine answer to any of these — sensible
defaults get applied and recorded as assumptions.

- **Budget** — is there one? Roughly what size, and who approves spend? At what amount does someone more
  senior have to say yes?
- **People** — what roles and rough headcount do you have, and any contractors or vendors? Is there a role or
  a person everything seems to wait on?
- **Approval points** — do projects pass through any formal gates or reviews before they proceed (funding,
  build, launch)? Is there a steering committee, and how often does it meet?
- **Risk appetite** — in plain English, where is the organization cautious and where is it willing to take a
  chance? (E.g. "we'll take schedule risk, never compliance risk.")
- **Change** — who decides when scope, dates, or budget need to move?

_Your answer here..._

### ✅ Done?
Run **`/setup-pmo`** and your PMO will validate your answers, seed its memory (`knowledge/`), confirm your
process conventions, and tell you what it can do next. From there, just ask in plain English — e.g.
*"build me a roadmap,"* *"write a PRD for X,"* *"plan next sprint,"* or *"give me a status report."*
