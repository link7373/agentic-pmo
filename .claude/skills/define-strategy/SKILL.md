# /define-strategy — Set product direction

## When to use
Setting or revisiting vision, positioning, business model, market sizing, or OKRs.

## Dispatches
`product-strategist` (lead). Consult `product-analyst` for outcome-metric definitions in key results.

## Inputs
`knowledge/product-context.md`, `knowledge/stakeholder-map.md`, any discovery findings.

## Steps
1. Confirm the vision and target customer; flag if missing or vague.
2. Draft positioning with the standard statement; pressure-test specificity and defensibility.
3. Capture the business model on one page; size the market bottom-up with stated assumptions.
4. Set OKRs whose key results measure outcomes (not output) and ladder to the strategy.
5. Name the few bets that matter and what you're deliberately not doing.

## Methods
`knowledge/methods/product-strategy.md`, `knowledge/methods/metrics-and-experimentation.md`.

## Output
Use `templates/okr.md` for the OKR section. Update `knowledge/product-context.md` (vision, positioning,
business model, OKRs); log the strategic
decision to `knowledge/decision-log.md`. Follow `standards/document-standards.md`.
