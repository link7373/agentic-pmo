# Resource Register

> **Who is available, to what, and how heavily committed.** Owned by `resource-manager`. Maintained by
> `/manage-resources`; consumed by `/plan-capacity` and `portfolio-analyst`'s demand-vs-capacity analytics.
>
> This is the **supply** side. Demand lives in `knowledge/portfolio.md` and the project plans; team velocity
> lives in `knowledge/cadence.md`. Keep them separate — a capacity conclusion that mixes the two silently is
> the most common way portfolio planning goes wrong.
>
> **Privacy:** this file names people. Treat it as internal-restricted. Never copy individual allocation or
> utilization rows into a stakeholder-facing deliverable or a dashboard — report by role or team instead
> (`standards/dashboard-standards.md`, privacy).
