# Intake & Triage Register

> The PMO's front door. Every inbound signal — customer feedback, support themes, sales asks, stakeholder
> requests, internal ideas, bug trends — lands here first via `/capture-feedback`, gets triaged, and is
> routed onward (to discovery, the backlog, the roadmap, or declined). Keeps the funnel honest and visible.

(→ `/build-roadmap`) · **Now/quick** (→ backlog top) · **Decline** (with reason) · **Watch** (not yet)._

## Recurring themes
_Patterns seen across many signals (e.g., "onboarding confusion" mentioned 12×). Themes are strong candidates
for discovery and roadmap opportunities — promote them deliberately._

## Declined / parked (with reason)
_Things we consciously chose not to pursue, and why — so they don't keep re-entering the funnel._
