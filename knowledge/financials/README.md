# Financial artifacts

Dated financial artifacts owned by `financial-analyst`. The living registers — cost baselines, actuals, EVM,
benefits — stay in `knowledge/financials.md`; this directory holds the point-in-time documents.

| Artifact | Skill | Naming | Template |
|----------|-------|--------|----------|
| Business case | `/build-business-case` | `YYYY-MM-DD-<initiative>-business-case.md` | `templates/business-case.md` |
| Variance / financial review | `/track-financials` | `YYYY-MM-DD-<period>-financial-review.md` | — |
| Benefits realization review | `/track-financials` | `YYYY-MM-DD-<initiative>-benefits-review.md` | — |

Every figure states whether it is measured or estimated, and every business case includes the do-nothing option.
A benefit claimed in a business case here must appear in the benefits register in `knowledge/financials.md`, or
nobody will ever be asked whether it arrived.
