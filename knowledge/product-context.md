# Product Context

> Source of truth for product direction. Seeded by `/setup-pmo` from `START-HERE.md`; refined by
> `/define-strategy`, `/run-discovery`, and `/build-roadmap`. Agents treat this as authoritative.

## Product / Portfolio
_What we're building, stage (idea / MVP / launched / scaling), and whether it's one product or several._

## Vision
_The long-term change we're working toward._

## Target customers & market
_Who we serve (specific segments), the problem we solve, alternatives/competitors, market notes._

## Positioning
_For [target customer] who [need], our [category] is [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], we [differentiation]._

## Business model
_Segments, value proposition, channels, revenue model, cost drivers, key resources/partners, moat._

## Market sizing
_TAM / SAM / SOM with bottom-up assumptions._

## Goals & OKRs
_Current objectives and key results (outcome-based). North-star metric + guardrails._

## Success measures
_How we know it's working (key metrics, definitions)._

## Validated learnings
_Key evidence from discovery: jobs-to-be-done, validated/invalidated assumptions, personas (links)._
