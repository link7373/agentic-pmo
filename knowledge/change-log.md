# Change Log

> **Every formal change to an approved baseline.** Maintained by `/manage-change`; owned by `project-manager`
> at project level and `program-manager` where a change crosses projects. Policy and thresholds come from
> `knowledge/governance.md`.
>
> A change is anything that moves approved **scope, schedule, or cost**. Work that fits inside the baseline is
> not a change — don't inflate this log with normal backlog churn. But a re-baseline that never gets recorded
> here makes every later variance report meaningless, so when in doubt, log it.
