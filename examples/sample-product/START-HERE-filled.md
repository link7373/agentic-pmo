## 1. The Product / Portfolio
Cadence — an AI assistant that turns async standup updates into a clean daily digest and flags blockers.
Stage: building the MVP. Vision: make status reporting effortless so teams spend meetings on decisions, not
recaps. One product for now.

## 2. Users & Market
Target: engineering managers and scrum masters at 20–200-person software companies running distributed
teams. Problem: standups eat time and updates get lost in chat. Alternatives: live standups, Slack threads,
generic note-takers. Market: crowded productivity space; we win on the blocker-detection + digest, not notes.

## 3. Goals & Metrics
This quarter: prove teams will adopt a daily AI digest. North star: weekly active teams that read the digest
≥4 days/week. Today we watch signups and digest open rate. Biggest unknown: do managers trust the
auto-detected blockers enough to act on them?

## 4. Teams & Stakeholders
8 people: 1 PM (me), 5 engineers, 1 designer, 1 founder/CEO (sponsor, final say on scope). One delivery team.
CEO is very hands-on on roadmap.

## 5. Process & Cadence
Scrum, 2-week sprints. We do planning, daily standup, review, retro. Release roughly weekly behind flags.
No hard external deadlines, but CEO wants a public beta in ~10 weeks.

## 6. Tools & Integrations
Backlog in Linear. Comms in Slack. Docs in Notion. Eventually we'd like the PMO to sync the backlog with
Linear and post status to Slack, but file-only is fine to start.

## 7. Rules & Boundaries
We handle customer message content — privacy matters; no training on customer data without consent. Don't
commit to customer-facing dates without CEO sign-off.

## 8. Context & Quirks
"Digest" = the daily summary. "Blocker score" = our confidence a item is blocked. Timezone: US Eastern.
We tried live video standups last year; team hated them. Fiscal year = calendar year.
