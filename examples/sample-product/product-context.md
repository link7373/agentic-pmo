## Product / Portfolio
Cadence — AI assistant that turns async standup updates into a daily digest and flags blockers. Stage: MVP.
Single product.

## Vision
Make status reporting effortless so teams spend meetings on decisions, not recaps.

## Target customers & market
Engineering managers and scrum masters at 20–200-person software companies with distributed teams.
Problem: standups cost time; updates get lost in chat. Alternatives: live standups, Slack threads, generic
note-takers. Crowded market; differentiation is blocker detection + digest, not transcription.

## Positioning
For engineering managers of distributed teams who lose time to standups and scattered updates, Cadence is a
daily AI digest that surfaces progress and blockers automatically. Unlike live standups or generic
note-takers, it tells you what's blocked, not just what was said.

## Business model
Segments: EM/scrum master buys; team uses. Value prop: reclaim meeting time, catch blockers early.
Channel: PLG self-serve + Slack/Linear integrations. Revenue: per-seat subscription. Moat (intended):
accumulated blocker-detection signal improves with usage.

## Goals & OKRs (2026 Q3)
**Objective:** Prove teams adopt the daily digest.
- KR1: Weekly active teams reading the digest ≥4 days/week → target 30 teams.
- KR2: Digest open rate → 35% → 60%.
- KR3: Blocker-flag action rate (flagged blockers a manager acts on) ≥ 40%.
North star: weekly active teams reading the digest ≥4 days/week. Guardrail: false-positive blocker rate < 20%.

## Success measures
Digest open rate, weekly active teams, blocker action rate, false-positive blocker rate.
